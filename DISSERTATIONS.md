# SpineBench: dissertations and theses

Theses with benchmark-quality data (full tables, raw curves, per-specimen results) or complete protocols. Each entry was checked against a repository or catalogue record: PolyPublie, MIT/KU/Guelph/Adelaide/McGill DSpace, OPARU Ulm, DepositOnce, Refubium, DataCite, DNB, ERA Alberta, the Twente and VU portals, or QUT ePrints. A spot-check of five links on 2026-11-11 (PolyPublie 4082 and 33503, MIT DSpace, Berkeley PDF, DepositOnce) resolved. PDFs were not opened, so "What it adds" is based on the abstract or record. Where no abstract existed it is inferred from the title and marked so.

**36 verified** theses, plus 13 that could not be verified (listed at the end, not to be relied on).

Open = full text freely downloadable from the link. "no" = record only (request from the library or the author).

## Highest value for SpineBench

- **Ghezelbash2019thesis**: Farshid Ghezelbash (2019), PhD, Polytechnique Montréal. *Subject-Specific Computational Musculoskeletal Modeling of Human Trunk in Lifting : Role of Age, Sex, Body Weight and Body Height*. Open: yes. Link: https://publications.polymtl.ca/4082/. Full description of the sex/age/body-height/body-weight scaling algorithm (muscle moment arms and CSAs, spine geometry, passive ligamentous properties, gravity loads) for the KD-FE trunk model with added T11-T12 disc, the full-factorial sensitivity design (BH 150-190 cm, BW 50-120 kg, age 35-60) and obesity-shape (waist circumference) mass distributions: candidate IN4/IN5 inputs and IR robustness rule.
- **Bayoglu2019thesis**: Riza Bayoglu (2019), PhD, University of Twente. *Twente Spine Model: Development, validation, and application of a complete and coherent musculoskeletal model of the human spine*. Open: yes. Link: https://doi.org/10.3990/1.9789036547710. The Twente Spine Dataset: digitised origin/insertion/via points and fibre length, tendon length, sarcomere length, optimal fibre length, pennation angle, mass and PCSA for every muscle-tendon element of the whole spine (one cadaver), plus a full-spine model with flexible rib cage validated on quasi-static trunk tasks. A core PC1 source for thoracic muscles.
- **Boudreault1994thesis**: Francis Boudreault (1994), MSc, École Polytechnique de Montréal. *Comportement mécanique des unités fonctionnelles : T3/T4, T7/T8 et T12/L1 saines et lésées du rachis humain*. Open: yes. Link: https://publications.polymtl.ca/33503/. Experimental thoracic FSU data: 17 FSUs from 8 cadaver spines (T3/T4 n=6, T7/T8 n=5, T12/L1 n=6) in flexion, extension, left/right lateral bending and left/right torsion, tested intact, without the articular joints, and further reduced (ligamentum flavum, interspinous, supraspinous), plus a preload effect. Thoracic sequential-resection data that is probably not in any journal paper.
- **Bruno2015thesis**: Alexander G. Bruno (2015), PhD, Massachusetts Institute of Technology (Harvard-MIT Program in Health Sciences and Technology). *Investigation of spine loading to understand vertebral fractures*. Open: yes. Link: https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/98727. Development and validation of the thoracolumbar MSK model against in vivo IDP, VBR telemetry and EMG, plus automated CT-based subject-specific methods (spine curvature, trunk muscle morphology) and population variation: full validation tables and CT muscle-morphology protocol.
- **Christophy2010thesis**: Miguel Christophy (2010), MSc, University of California, Berkeley (Mechanical Engineering). *A Detailed Open-Source Musculoskeletal Model of the Human Lumbar Spine*. Open: yes. Link: https://spine.berkeley.edu/papers/Miguel-Masters.pdf. Full thesis-level documentation of the OpenSim lumbar model later published as Christophy et al. 2012 BMMB (238 fascicles, 8 muscle groups): muscle-path and architecture tables and segment mass assumptions.
- **Dreischarf2017thesis**: Marcel Dreischarf (2017), PhD, Technische Universität Berlin. *The loading, shape and motion of the lumbar spine*. Open: yes. Link: https://doi.org/10.14279/depositonce-6157. Cumulative thesis: VBR telemetry loads in 265 measurements from 5 patients (upper-body bending, lifting techniques), Epionics lordosis/ROM in n=323 (short-term) and n=208 (long-term) asymptomatic subjects by age and sex, and the 8-group lumbar FE model comparison: one source for M3, IN1/IN5 and F9.
- **Mannen2014thesis**: Erin M. Mannen (2014), PhD, University of Kansas. *Advancing Thoracic Spine Biomechanical Research*. Open: yes. Link: https://hdl.handle.net/1808/18375. Validation of the KU spine test machine (error <2 deg), cadaveric thoracic spine ROM/stiffness with intact rib cage, and sequential Ponte osteotomies. Full per-step tables for thoracic + rib cage resection.
- **Naserkhaki2016thesis**: Sadegh Naserkhaki (2016), PhD, University of Alberta. *On the Lumbosacral Spine Geometry Variation and Spinal Load-Sharing: Personalized Finite Element Modelling*. Open: yes. Link: https://doi.org/10.7939/r3hm52q8q. Three geometrically personalised L1-S1 FE models (hypo-, normal-, hyper-lordotic) under follower load + moments, validated against in vitro/in vivo data, with load-sharing among disc, ligaments and facets. Probably also holds full ligament/disc property tables.
- **Sis2016thesis**: Hadley L. Sis (2016), MSc, University of Kansas. *Evaluation of a Follower Load with an Intact Rib Cage*. Open: yes. Link: https://hdl.handle.net/1808/21980. Thoracic spine with intact rib cage under a follower load, evaluated with all common motion and stiffness parameters (ROM, NZ, stiffness). Thoracic follower-load target data.
- **Zwambag2016thesis**: Derek P. Zwambag (2016), PhD, University of Guelph. *Investigation of intrinsic spine muscle properties to improve musculoskeletal spine modelling*. Open: yes. Link: https://hdl.handle.net/10214/10144. Passive muscle stress-length data (animal controls, facet-injury model), sarcomere lengths of human spine muscles in neutral posture measured by laser diffraction on cadavers (posterior shorter than anterior) and their posture dependence, plus a flexion-relaxation-capable muscle model. Normative architecture inputs. (Name is Derek, not Daniel.)
