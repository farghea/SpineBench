# SpineBench: papers needing Georgia Tech library access

71 papers in total: 32 from the in vivo/MSK list and 39 from the in vitro/FE list. No paper appears in both. IDs and suite codes match `papers_all.csv` and `PLAN.md` (M = MSK suites, F = FE suites). The list is sorted by tier, then by first suite.

How to get them: GT Library EZproxy. Use interlibrary loan for the pre-1980 JBJS and J Biomech Eng papers. Before using GT for the group papers (G01, G03-G09), check IRSST PHARESST in a browser; many IRSST-funded papers have author manuscripts there. Lund 1998 may be free in the Bone & Joint archive; check before requesting it.

Status: 📂 = open in Chrome, ✅ = downloaded (PDF in `donwload/`, renamed to its ID), ❌ = no access, not downloaded.
