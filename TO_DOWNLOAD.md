# SpineBench: papers to download

Built 2026-11-11 from `papers_all.csv` (column `pdf_in_folder`). Every paper below was checked against Crossref (DOI resolves, first author and year match), and tier 1-2 papers also against the PubMed abstract or publisher page. Open-access links come from OpenAlex/PMC/repositories and were not all clicked; if one fails, use the DOI through the GT library.

**How to use:** download, save as `donwload/<uid>.pdf` (the uid is the first column, e.g. `Fujimori2014.pdf`), tick the box. Tier 1 = a suite cannot run without it; tier 2 = strong supporting data; tier 3 = optional context or bound check (download last, or skip).
