# Access list (Georgia Tech library): SpineBench in vivo/MSK

These papers had no open copy I could find. IDs match `papers.csv`. They are ordered by tier, then ID.

- Before you use GT, check IRSST PHARESST (pharesst.irsst.qc.ca) for the group papers (G01, G03-G09). Many IRSST-funded papers have author manuscripts there. The site blocks scripts but works in a browser.
- Your own papers (U01-U12) are not listed here because you have them. U12 (JB 2020, NIOSH) had no open copy listed.
- Your 2019 PhD thesis has the full protocol of the 19-subject cohort: https://publications.polymtl.ca/4082/1/2019_FarshidGhezelbash.pdf (open access; it timed out from this machine).
